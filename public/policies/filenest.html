Privacy Policy

Our privacy approach

Filenest follows a local-first approach. Core file processing takes place on your device. We do not routinely collect your personal files or monitor your normal application activities.

Uniquator ("We", "Our", or "Us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how Filenest ("App") collects, processes, stores, and shares information when you use the application and its associated services.

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Significant changes will be communicated through appropriate notification channels within five (5) days of the update.

1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

  • Uniquator: The developer and operator of Filenest.
  • App: Refers to the Filenest Android application.
  • User, You, or Your: Refers to an individual using the App.
  • Personal Information: Information that identifies or can reasonably be associated with an individual.
  • Third-Party Services: External services, platforms, APIs, and infrastructure providers integrated into or accessed by the App.

2. COLLECTION AND PROCESSING OF INFORMATION

2.1 Normal Application Usage

Uniquator does not routinely collect personal information or monitor users' activities during normal application usage.

Filenest is designed to process files and perform its core functionalities locally on your device.

We do not routinely collect information about:

  • Files selected for processing.
  • Documents viewed or edited within the App.
  • Images selected for processing.
  • Device status indicators, such as battery percentage.
  • Application appearance preferences, including Dark Mode and Light Mode.

Certain features require internet connectivity and involve limited information processing, as described below.

2.2 Bug Reports and Diagnostic Information

When a user generates and submits a bug report, the report may include:

  • Public IP address (IPv4 or IPv6).
  • Device manufacturer and model.
  • Android version and operating system information.
  • Hardware specifications.
  • Available storage information.
  • Network-related diagnostic information.
  • Other relevant technical information required to investigate the reported issue.

This information is used for:

  • Investigating reported bugs.
  • Identifying technical problems.
  • Diagnosing compatibility issues.
  • Improving application stability and performance.

Diagnostic information is not routinely collected through this reporting mechanism without the relevant bug-report action.

2.3 Weekly IP Lookup and Regional Compliance

Filenest may periodically perform an IP geolocation lookup, generally once per week, to determine the approximate geographical region of the user.

The process involves:

  1. Obtaining the user's public IP address.
  2. Sending the IP address to a trusted third-party IP geolocation API.
  3. Receiving approximate geographical information, such as the country or state.
  4. Using the returned information to determine applicable regional requirements and application availability.

Purpose of Regional Processing

Regional information may be used for:

  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations in different countries.
  • Identifying countries or regions where Filenest or specific functionalities may be restricted or unavailable.
  • Enforcing country-specific application restrictions.
  • Enabling or disabling features available only in certain geographical regions.
  • Applying regional service availability requirements.
  • Supporting other region-dependent application functionality.

Location Accuracy:

  • We use IP-based geolocation rather than GPS positioning for this functionality.
  • We do not use this feature to determine precise physical coordinates.
  • IP-based geolocation may provide inaccurate or outdated regional information.
  • Third-party geolocation providers may independently process IP addresses under their own policies.

Regional Privacy Control

Users located in the European Union (EU) and the State of California, United States, can disable the optional weekly IP lookup through the available application setting.

When disabled, Filenest will not initiate the optional weekly IP geolocation lookup.

Disabling this functionality does not disable independent network operations, including update checks, privacy-policy checks, Special Day requests, bug report submissions, or other application services.

2.4 Periodic Online Checks and Network Communications

Filenest may periodically connect to the internet to retrieve information necessary for maintaining, updating, and operating the application.

These communications may include:

  • Application Update Checker: Periodically contacts Uniquator's infrastructure to check whether a newer application version is available.
  • Privacy Policy Checker: Retrieves information about the current Privacy Policy version to identify revisions.
  • Regional Availability Checks: Retrieves information about regional restrictions, feature availability, and applicable requirements.
  • Other Operational Checks: Retrieves configuration information and supports application maintenance and associated services.

These communications may expose standard network information, including the user's IP address, to the relevant servers and service providers.

2.5 Special Day Feature

Filenest includes a Special Day feature that displays information about selected special days, occasions, or events at the top of the application.

To provide this functionality:

  • The App connects to Uniquator's server infrastructure hosted through GitHub.
  • It retrieves a list of special days and related information.
  • The retrieved information is used to determine whether a special day should be displayed.
  • Retrieved information may be temporarily cached locally to improve performance and reduce unnecessary network requests.

This functionality does not require access to your personal files, contacts, or precise GPS location.

The hosting infrastructure may process standard network information, including IP addresses, when the App requests the list. GitHub's own privacy and data-handling practices apply.

The feature may not display updated information when an internet connection is unavailable.

2.6 Third-Party Advertising Services

Filenest may integrate Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising technologies may independently collect or process certain information depending on the advertising configuration, applicable consent, and user settings.

Such information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device information.
  • IP addresses.
  • Advertising interaction information.
  • Information required for advertising delivery and measurement.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • Google Ads Data and Privacy

2.7 Google Drive Integration

If you choose to back up or transfer files using Google Drive functionality, the selected files are transferred to and stored on Google's infrastructure.

This operation is initiated through the relevant user-facing functionality.

Files stored through Google Drive are subject to Google's privacy, security, and storage practices. Uniquator does not claim ownership of your files stored in your Google account.

3. INFORMATION PROCESSED LOCALLY

Filenest is designed to perform its core file-processing operations locally on your device.

The following information is generally processed locally and is not routinely transmitted to Uniquator's servers:

  • Files selected for processing.
  • PDF processing information.
  • Image processing information.
  • Temporary files generated during operations.
  • Device status indicators.
  • Application appearance preferences.
  • Other temporary application data required for local functionality.

Information may leave your device when you explicitly use a feature requiring uploading, sharing, backing up, or submitting information, or when a feature requires the network communications described in this policy.

4. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY PROCESSING

Uniquator does not sell users' personal information. We may rely on external service providers to operate certain application functionalities.

4.1 IP Geolocation Providers

When weekly IP lookup is enabled, the user's public IP address is sent to a third-party geolocation API to retrieve approximate regional information.

The provider may process the IP address according to its own privacy policy and retention practices.

4.2 GitHub Infrastructure

Filenest uses GitHub-hosted infrastructure to retrieve information for features such as Special Day.

The hosting infrastructure may process standard network information, including IP addresses, as part of handling requests.

4.3 Google Services

Google services may be used for advertising and optional Google Drive integration. Information shared with Google depends on the functionality being used and the relevant service configuration.

4.4 No Unnecessary File Transfers

Normal local file-processing operations do not require uploading users' selected files to Uniquator's servers.

Files are transferred externally only when a feature explicitly requires it, such as a user-initiated backup or sharing operation.

5. DATA PROTECTION AND STORAGE

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5.1 Company Servers

Information received through bug reports and other application services may be stored on infrastructure operated by Uniquator or its service providers.

5.2 Local Storage

Information processed locally remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to share, export, or back it up through an available feature.

5.3 Third-Party Storage

Information processed through Google, GitHub, and other integrated service providers may be stored or processed on their respective infrastructure. Their own privacy and security practices apply.

5.4 Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce relevant policies.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps will be taken to delete or anonymize it, where applicable.

6. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information collected or processed through Filenest may be used for:

  • Investigating and resolving technical problems.
  • Improving application stability and performance.
  • Determining approximate geographical regions.
  • Enforcing regional restrictions and applicable legal requirements.
  • Determining regional feature availability.
  • Checking application update availability.
  • Checking Privacy Policy revisions.
  • Providing the Special Day feature.
  • Maintaining application infrastructure.
  • Supporting advertising functionality.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not intentionally use locally processed files for advertising, profiling, or statistical analysis.

7. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have the following rights:

7.1 Right to Access

You may request information about personal information processed by Uniquator.

7.2 Right to Correction

You may request correction of inaccurate personal information associated with your records.

7.3 Right to Erasure

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your identifiable records, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7.4 Withdrawal of Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time.

Withdrawing consent may affect certain functionalities that depend on the relevant processing.

7.5 Regional IP Lookup Control

Users located in the European Union and California can disable the optional weekly IP lookup through the available application setting.

This setting controls the optional weekly geolocation operation and does not automatically disable unrelated network communications.

7.6 Additional Rights

Where applicable, users may also have rights relating to:

  • Restriction of processing.
  • Objection to certain processing activities.
  • Data portability.
  • Opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information, where applicable.
  • Limiting certain uses of sensitive personal information, where applicable.

These rights depend on the applicable jurisdiction and whether the relevant legal requirements apply to Uniquator.

7.7 Limitations

Certain technical information, including IP addresses processed through network infrastructure and third-party services, may not always be independently identifiable or retrievable for an individual deletion request.

Requests will be evaluated and handled in accordance with applicable laws and technical limitations.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable requirements, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information.

Where applicable, we comply with relevant age-related privacy, parental consent, and child-protection requirements.
