1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Uniquator ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the Filenest application ("App").

It applies to information processed through the App and its associated services and features.

2. Overview

Our approach: Filenest is designed with local processing in mind. Core file operations are performed on your device rather than routinely uploading your personal files to Uniquator servers.

We do not sell users' personal information. Some features, however, require internet connections or may involve third-party services. Those operations are explained below.

3. Information We Process

3.1 Normal app usage

During normal use, Uniquator does not routinely collect your personal files or the content you process using Filenest. Core processing takes place locally on your device.

3.2 Device and technical information

Certain features, particularly bug reporting and troubleshooting, may process technical information such as:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Android version and operating system information
  • Hardware specifications
  • Available device storage
  • Network or signal information
  • Device identifiers, where available and relevant
  • IP address and diagnostic information

The exact information depends on the feature being used and the information available on your device.

3.3 Network information

When the App connects to online services, those services may receive standard connection information, including your IP address, as part of ordinary internet communication.

4. Local Processing and Device Data

Filenest processes supported files and documents locally on your device. This includes file operations such as PDF and image processing.

Temporary files, selected files, and other working data may be created or stored locally to provide the requested functionality.

App preferences, such as theme settings, and device indicators used by the interface are generally handled locally.

These local operations do not ordinarily transmit the contents of your files to Uniquator. This does not apply to features where you explicitly initiate an upload, backup, sharing operation, or another network-based action.

5. Internet Connections and Background Requests

Although core file processing is local, Filenest may periodically connect to the internet for operational purposes.

These connections may include:

  • Update checker: Checks for information about available app updates.
  • Privacy policy checker: Checks the current privacy policy version or whether policy information has changed.
  • Regional availability checks: Helps determine whether certain features are available in a particular region.
  • Maintenance requests: Other requests needed to maintain or operate app functionality.

These requests may expose standard network information, including an IP address, to the relevant service provider. The services involved may maintain their own connection logs under their respective policies.

6. Regional Availability and Weekly IP Lookup

Filenest may periodically, generally once a week, use an IP geolocation service to estimate your approximate region, such as your country or state.

This lookup helps Uniquator:

  • Identify regions where the App or particular features may be restricted.
  • Determine regional availability of features.
  • Apply region-specific product settings and restrictions.
  • Support consideration of applicable country-specific requirements.

IP-based geolocation is approximate. It does not provide your precise GPS location and may not always identify your actual location correctly.

Regional control

Users in the European Union and California can disable this weekly IP lookup through the available App setting.

Important: Disabling the weekly IP lookup does not disable other independent network operations, such as update checks, privacy policy checks, Special Day requests, or bug reports.

7. Bug Reports and Diagnostics

If you generate or submit a bug report, the report may include technical diagnostic information and your IP address.

Diagnostic information may include your device model, Android version, signal strength, available storage, hardware specifications, device identifiers, and other relevant troubleshooting information.

This information is used to investigate errors, identify compatibility issues, diagnose performance problems, and improve Filenest.

Please review the information included in a bug report before submitting it, particularly if it contains information you do not want to share.

8. Special Day Feature

Filenest may display selected special days, occasions, or events through its Special Day feature.

To provide this feature, the App may contact a Uniquator service hosted using GitHub infrastructure to retrieve a list of relevant occasions. Some information may be cached locally on your device.

This feature does not require access to your personal files, contacts, or precise GPS location.

Requests to GitHub-hosted infrastructure may expose your IP address and other standard HTTP connection information. GitHub's own privacy policy and practices apply to its processing of that information.

The feature may not refresh while your device is offline.

9. Advertising and Google AdMob

Filenest may display advertisements using Google AdMob or related Google advertising services.

Depending on the advertising configuration, your region, consent choices, and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • IP address
  • Advertising interactions and impressions
  • Information used for fraud prevention and ad measurement

Advertising personalization and data processing may be subject to applicable consent requirements and available device or regional controls.

Google handles information under its own policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

10. Google Drive and Backups

If you choose to use a Google Drive backup, transfer, or related integration, the files and information you select may be uploaded to or accessed through Google Drive.

This action is initiated by you. Google processes information associated with your Google account and Drive activity under its own privacy policy and terms.

Uniquator does not control Google's independent data processing practices.

Read Google's Privacy Policy

11. Sharing of Information

Uniquator does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary to operate a feature or service, including:

  • Google advertising and AdMob services
  • Google Drive, when you initiate a Drive operation
  • GitHub-hosted infrastructure used for the Special Day feature
  • IP geolocation providers used for regional availability checks
  • Services used to receive or process diagnostic reports

Such providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable contractual arrangements. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the App.

12. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Local files and temporary data may remain on your device until you delete them or the App removes them as part of its normal operation.

Third-party providers may retain connection logs, diagnostic information, or other data according to their own retention practices and policies.

13. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed in connection with Filenest.

However, no application, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information processed about you. These may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion or erasure.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Requesting restriction of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Objecting to certain processing activities.
  • Requesting data portability where applicable.
  • Exercising applicable California privacy rights, including opt-out rights where relevant.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. You can contact us using the details below to submit a privacy request.

15. Children's Privacy

Filenest is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable requirements, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation, including deletion where appropriate.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, its features, service providers, or applicable requirements.

Users will be notified of significant changes within five (5) days of the update.

The effective date and last updated date at the beginning of this page indicate the relevant policy version.
