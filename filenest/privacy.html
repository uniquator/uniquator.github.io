Filenest Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 22, 2026

1. Overview

Filenest is designed as a browser-based PDF and image tool. The application processes files locally in your browser wherever technically possible. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data.

2. No Personal Data Collection

Filenest does not require user accounts, names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, or payment information. We do not intentionally collect personal information from users of the web application.

3. File Processing

Files selected by users are processed in the browser using client-side JavaScript libraries. Filenest does not upload your selected PDF or image files to a Filenest server for processing through this static web application.

4. Local Browser Data

Filenest may store limited preferences in your browser, such as whether you dismissed an Android app prompt. This information remains on your device and is used only to improve your local experience.

5. Third-Party Libraries and Links

Filenest may load client-side libraries, fonts, icons, or assets from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive standard technical information from your browser, such as IP address, user agent, and requested resource URL, according to their own policies. External links, including the About page and Android app download link, are governed by their respective sites.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Filenest does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, behavioral tracking, or profiling technologies in this static web application.

7. Children's Privacy

Filenest is not intended to collect information from children. Because no account system or personal data collection is used, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Data Security

Because files are processed locally in your browser, you remain responsible for the files you choose to open, process, save, or download. Use Filenest only on devices and browsers you trust.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates should be reflected by changing the effective date on this page.

10. Contact

For project information, visit the About page at bhaveshrohilla.github.io.
